Interests

Things I spend my time and money on, in no particular order.

Music

I've logged about 36,700 songs on Last.fm so far in 2026, around 117 a day, which puts me on track to beat last year's 41,332.

Muse is still my number one, same as last year, and Absolution is still my most played album (their best album btw). The real story of this year is Queens of the Stone Age. Almost all of my plays of them came in the past 12 months, and ...Like Clockwork went straight to my second most played album.

The rest is mostly me refusing to leave my emo phase. Panic! at the Disco sits at number two, or should I say got dethroned, because back in 2018 to 2020 they owned the top spot. My Chemical Romance is still up there too. Perunggu stayed on repeat, especially Kalibata, 2012 and Gemilang. Lately things have been getting heavier, with Loathe, Knocked Loose and Deafheaven showing up more and more.

Then there’s post-rock. Oh Hiroshima, I Am Waiting for You Last Summer, There’s a Light and Tides From Nebula have been in my rotation since last year, and I’ve been finding more post-rock bands this year than ever before. The latest one is Endless Dive, and they’ve been a lot of fun to listen to.

Movies

I've logged 272 films on Letterboxd, 14 of them this year. I rate most of them highly though. My ratings are very subjective and most of them are based on my personal connection to the story or characters instead of objective criteria.

My four favourites are The Dark Knight Rises, Arrival, Soul and Project Hail Mary. Look Back gets me too. I've watched it three times and it hits harder every time.

This year though, I've been watching more series than movies. I've watched the ones that have been on my watchlist for ages, and it's been fun.

Games

Mostly the story-heavy games.

Elden Ring

Unemployed me would appreciate this game even more.

I picked it up right after one of the shittiest phases of my entire life, so I might be SUPER biased here. But it really is one of those games that helps you get through things, you know? It still has a special spot in my brain.

The soundtrack, the exploration, the bosses, and hell, the scenery. All of it is beautiful and so engaging. I still remember the first time I set foot on the Altus Plateau. I just sat there in awe, staring at the Erdtree like an idiot. Hauntingly beautiful. Shaman Village too. I love that place.

As much as I love Elden Ring, what I hate the most is how little it actually tells you about its own story. But that’s also what gets so many people talking about it. And I love watching people try to piece everything together and land on the most unhinged conclusions possible.
