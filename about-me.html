About me

I’m a full-stack developer from West Java, Indonesia. I’ve been writing code for about three years now, one year freelancing and the rest at a company where I currently lead a small team. Most of my days go into healthcare systems. Hospital stuff, pharmacy modules, patient data, and a lot of BPJS and SATUSEHAT integration. I mostly work with Laravel, and I spend more time in PostgreSQL and SQL Server queries than I’d like to admit. Sometimes I also end up in actual server rooms, which is a nice break from staring at a screen.

Outside of work, I build things for fun. Right now I’m working on a multiplayer jigsaw puzzle game you can play with a friend or two check it out.

I shoot film on a half-frame Ektar H35. It’s way too expensive for how mid my photos come out, and half my rolls come back blank, but I’m committed to the bit. I also play guitar, well, learning to be exact. I bought it this year after months of overthinking, and now I write small songs for myself

Music takes up a big part of my life. Muse, Queens of the Stone Age, Linkin Park, a lot of post-rock.

That’s pretty much me. Thanks for stopping by.
